Roofing FAQs

Not sure what your roof needs? Here are answers to some of the questions we commonly receive.

How do I know if my roof needs repairing?

Signs can include missing or damaged tiles, leaks, damp patches, damaged flashing and visible wear. If you are unsure about a problem, having it assessed can help prevent further damage.

Can a damaged roof be repaired rather than replaced?

In many cases, yes. The right approach depends on the condition of the roof and the extent of the damage. Smaller problems can often be dealt with without replacing the entire roof.

Do you deal with emergency roof leaks?

Yes. If you have an unexpected leak or weather-related roof damage, get in touch and explain what has happened so the appropriate next steps can be discussed.

Do you repair flat roofs?

Yes. Flat roofs can develop leaks, damaged coverings and problems around joints or drainage. The appropriate repair depends on the condition of the existing roof.

Can you help with guttering problems?

Yes. Blocked, damaged or leaking guttering can cause water to overflow and create problems around the roofline. Guttering issues can be assessed as part of the overall problem.

How quickly can a repair be carried out?

This depends on the type and extent of the repair and current availability. Once the problem has been assessed, the required work and expected timescale can be discussed.

Do you provide quotes?

Yes. Contact us with some details about the problem and we can discuss the next steps and the work that may be required.

What areas do you cover?

We provide roofing services in Sheffield and surrounding areas. Contact us with your location to discuss whether we can help.
