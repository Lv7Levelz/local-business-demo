Frequently Asked Questions

If you are unsure about a roofing problem, these answers can help you understand what may be involved before arranging an inspection.

How do I know if my roof needs repairing?

Common signs include missing or damaged tiles, water coming into the property, damp patches, damaged flashing, blocked gutters or visible areas of wear. If you are unsure, it is worth having the roof inspected before a small issue becomes a larger repair.

Can you repair a roof rather than replace it?

In many cases, yes. The appropriate solution depends on the condition of the roof and the extent of the damage. A repair may be suitable where the problem is limited to a particular area.

Do you deal with emergency roof leaks?

Yes. If your roof has suddenly developed a leak or suffered weather-related damage, contact us to discuss the problem and arrange the appropriate response.

How quickly can a roof repair be carried out?

Timescales depend on the type of repair, the extent of the damage and availability. Once the problem has been assessed, you can discuss the work and expected timescale before proceeding.

Do you repair flat roofs?

Yes. Flat roof problems can include damaged coverings, leaks, worn edges and issues around joints or drainage. The right repair depends on the condition of the existing roof.

Can blocked gutters cause roof problems?

They can contribute to water overflowing and potentially cause problems around the roofline and walls. Keeping gutters clear helps water drain away properly.

Do you provide roofing quotes?

Yes. Contact us with details of the problem and we can discuss the next steps and the work that may be required.

What areas do you cover?

We provide roofing services in Sheffield and surrounding areas. Contact us with your location to discuss whether we can help.
