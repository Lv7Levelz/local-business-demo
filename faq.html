FAQs

Common roofing questions

If you can't find the answer you're looking for, get in touch and we'll be happy to discuss your requirements.

How do I know if my roof needs repairing?

Signs can include missing or damaged tiles, visible leaks, damp patches, damaged flashing, blocked gutters or water entering the property. Some problems may not be obvious from ground level, so professional inspection can help identify potential issues.

Can you help with a leaking roof?

Yes. If your roof is leaking, the source of the problem should be identified as soon as practical. Depending on the cause, repairs may involve tiles, flashing, roof coverings, guttering or other areas of the roof.

Do you repair flat roofs?

Yes. Flat roof repairs can include addressing leaks, damaged coverings, failed joints and other issues affecting the roof. The appropriate repair will depend on the type and condition of the roof.

Can you repair damaged guttering?

Yes. Guttering can suffer from blockages, leaks, damaged sections, loose fittings and poor drainage. These issues can allow water to reach areas of the building where it can cause further problems.

What are fascias and soffits?

Fascias and soffits form part of a property's roofline. They help protect the edge of the roof and provide a finished appearance around the roofline. Damaged or deteriorating sections may need repair or replacement.

Do you deal with emergency roofing problems?

We can discuss urgent roofing problems such as unexpected leaks, storm-related damage or damaged roof coverings and advise on the next appropriate step.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover Sheffield and surrounding areas, including areas such as Hillsborough, Ecclesall, Crookes, Dore, Meadowhead, Walkley, Broomhill, Sharrow and Handsworth. We also work in nearby areas, so contact us if you're unsure whether your location is covered.

How can I arrange a roofing job?

The easiest way is to get in touch by phone or email and explain what is happening with your roof. Providing photographs can also be useful when discussing a visible problem.

Have you got an urgent roofing problem?

If water is entering your property or your roof has suffered unexpected damage, contact us as soon as practical to discuss the situation.
