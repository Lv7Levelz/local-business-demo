Roofing FAQs

Roofing problems can raise a lot of questions. Here are some straightforward answers to common questions about roofing work.

If you cannot find the information you need, get in touch and explain what is happening with your roof.

01

How do I know if my roof needs repairing?

Common signs include missing or damaged tiles, leaks, damp patches, damaged flashing and visible wear. If you are unsure about a problem, having it assessed can help identify what needs attention.

02

Can a roof be repaired rather than replaced?

In many cases, yes. The appropriate solution depends on the condition of the roof and the extent of the damage. Smaller problems may only require a targeted repair.

03

Do you deal with emergency roof leaks?

Yes. If your roof has developed a sudden leak or suffered weather-related damage, contact us with details of the problem so the appropriate next steps can be discussed.

04

Do you repair flat roofs?

Yes. Flat roofs can develop leaks, damaged coverings and problems around joints or drainage. The right repair depends on the condition of the existing roof.

05

Can you help with guttering problems?

Yes. Blocked, damaged or leaking guttering can cause water to overflow and create problems around the roofline. Guttering issues can be assessed as part of the overall problem.

06

How quickly can a roof repair be carried out?

Timescales depend on the type and extent of the repair and current availability. Once the problem has been assessed, the required work and expected timescale can be discussed.

07

Do you provide roofing quotes?

Yes. Get in touch with some details about the roofing problem and the next steps and potential work can be discussed.

08

What areas do you cover?

Roofing services are available across Sheffield and surrounding areas. Contact us with your location to discuss whether the service is suitable for your property.
